Upwell

Create for your audience. We'll bring it to the surface.

Privacy policy · Terms of service

Upwell is a desktop app for Windows that helps independent video creators get from raw footage to a published video without the busywork.

  • Trim and export your videos locally: crop for Shorts or widescreen, fade, adjust volume.
  • Write titles, descriptions, hashtags, and tags in your own voice, checked against each platform's rules.
  • Pick thumbnail frames from your video.
  • Publish to your YouTube channel directly from the app, as private, unlisted, public, or scheduled.
  • Post to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels from the same place, with each platform's own caption and settings.
  • Plan and schedule your lives on YouTube and Twitch: write the title and description, pick a thumbnail, and schedule it, so you just press Start Streaming in OBS.
  • After a live, mark the best moments for chapters, refresh the replay, and cut Shorts from your recording.
  • See how your videos do: views, click-through, watch time, and live viewers from YouTube Analytics, and which title habits go with your best videos.

Yours, locally

Upwell runs on your own computer. Your videos and your library stay there. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose a video and press a publish button, and then it goes only to your own accounts. There are no Upwell servers, accounts, ads, or tracking.

YouTube

When you connect a YouTube channel, Upwell asks Google for permission to upload videos, read your channel's name, and manage your lives (schedule them and set their titles, tags, and thumbnails). Upwell never deletes anything on YouTube. You can disconnect at any time in Upwell's settings or at your Google Account's connections page.

TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook

Connect your own TikTok account to post a video or send it to your TikTok drafts, and your Instagram professional account and Facebook Page to post Reels. You choose the caption, who can watch, and every setting before anything is sent. Disconnect any time in Upwell's settings.
