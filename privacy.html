Privacy policy

Effective November 26, 2026 (updated the same day to cover scheduling lives)

Upwell is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your computer, and Upwell only sends something somewhere when you ask it to.

What stays on your computer

  • Your videos. Upwell reads them where they are and never changes the originals.
  • Your library: video details, titles, descriptions, tags, notes, exports, and upload history, kept in a local database in your Windows user folder.
  • Channel statistics you import from a YouTube Studio export file.
  • Sign-in credentials (your Google sign-in token and any API key you add), kept in Windows Credential Manager on your computer.

There are no Upwell servers. The developer never receives your videos, your library, your credentials, or any usage data. Upwell has no analytics, ads, or tracking.

Google and YouTube data

If you connect a YouTube channel, Upwell asks Google for three permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the video file, title, description, tags, settings, and thumbnail you choose, when you press Upload.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to show which channel is connected (its name and handle).
  • Manage your YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl): to schedule the lives you plan in Upwell, connect them to your stream key, and set their title, description, tags, category, and thumbnail, and to read your stream keys' names so you can pick one. Upwell only changes what you ask it to, when you press Schedule or Save, and only on lives and videos you published from Upwell.

Upwell never deletes anything on YouTube, even though the "manage" permission technically allows it. It can't access anything else in your Google account, such as Gmail or Drive. The information Google returns (your channel's name, your stream keys' names, and the IDs of the videos and lives you publish) is stored only on your computer and used only to show you what's connected and link to what you've published. Upwell never stores or shows your stream key itself. None of it is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Upwell's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Optional AI writing help

If you add your own Anthropic API key and choose "Write it with Claude", Upwell sends the text details you entered about that video (for example the subject, style, and notes), your brand's writing guidelines, and example titles to Anthropic to draft a title and description. It doesn't send your video files. That request is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy. If you don't add a key, nothing is sent.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect YouTube in Upwell's settings. This revokes Upwell's access and deletes the stored sign-in token. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling Upwell and removing its data folder (%APPDATA%\app.upwell.desktop). Your original videos are never touched.
